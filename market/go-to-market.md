# Go-To-Market Strategy

This document outlines how SARE is introduced, adopted, and expanded within customer environments.

SARE’s go-to-market strategy emphasizes **trust-first adoption**, incremental value delivery, and alignment with existing SOC workflows.

---

## Entry Point Strategy

SARE is introduced as a **non-disruptive enhancement** to Microsoft Sentinel.

Initial deployment focuses on:
- Read-only insights
- Decision summaries
- Evidence aggregation

No automated execution is required at onboarding.

---

## Adoption Phases

### Phase 1 — Observe & Assist
- Deploy SARE in read-only mode
- Generate decision summaries
- Provide analyst guidance
- No approval or execution required

Goal:
- Build analyst trust
- Demonstrate value quickly

---

### Phase 2 — Governed Automation
- Enable approval-gated actions
- Integrate Sentinel playbooks
- Apply guardrails and audit logging

Goal:
- Reduce response time safely
- Maintain human accountability

---

### Phase 3 — Scaled Execution
- Expand approved action catalog
- Apply automation to high-volume cases
- Tune decision thresholds

Goal:
- Operational efficiency at scale

---
