# Pricing Model

This document outlines a realistic and scalable pricing approach for SARE.

The goal is to align pricing with **value delivered**, **enterprise expectations**, and **low adoption friction**.

---

## Pricing Philosophy

- Pricing should scale with usage and impact
- Entry barriers should be low
- Governance and safety are premium differentiators
- Customers should not pay per-alert unpredictably

---

## Primary Pricing Model

### Tiered Subscription (Recommended)

#### Starter (Read-Only / Assistive)
- Decision summaries
- Evidence aggregation
- AI-assisted analyst support
- No automated execution

**Indicative Price:**  
$500–$1,000 / month per Sentinel workspace

---

#### Professional (Governed Automation)
- Everything in Starter
- Approval-gated automation
- Sentinel playbook integration
- Audit logging and reporting

**Indicative Price:**  
$2,000–$5,000 / month per workspace

---

#### Enterprise (Advanced Governance)
- Everything in Professional
- Custom guardrails
- Extended retention and reporting
- Priority support

**Indicative Price:**  
Custom pricing

---

## Alternative Pricing Options (Optional)

### Usage-Based Add-ons
- Additional enrichment volume
- Extended AI assistance
- Premium threat intelligence sources

### MSSP / Multi-Tenant Licensing
- Per-tenant pricing
- Volume discounts
- Centralized governance dashboards

---

## Cost Considerations

Key cost drivers:
- AI inference usage
- Enrichment query volume
- Data retention
- Support and maintenance

SARE minimizes cost by:
- Bounded AI usage
- Deterministic logic first
- Reuse of Sentinel-native capabilities

---

## Competitive Positioning

Compared to:
- Traditional SOAR: SARE is safer and more governed
- AI-only tools: SARE is explainable and auditable

Pricing reflects **trust, control, and reliability**, not just automation.

---

## Key Takeaway

SARE pricing is designed to encourage adoption while reflecting its value as a **governed, enterprise-ready AI capability**.
