# Market Strategy

This document defines the market positioning, target customers, and value proposition for SARE (Sentinel Autonomous Response Engine).

SARE is positioned as an **AI-assisted governance and decision layer** for Microsoft Sentinel environments.

---

## Market Problem

Security Operations Centers (SOCs) face increasing challenges:

- Alert and incident volume continues to grow
- Skilled analyst capacity does not scale linearly
- Automation exists but is often risky or over-permissive
- AI tools lack governance, explainability, and trust

Many organizations either:
- Over-automate and accept risk, or
- Avoid automation and accept inefficiency

SARE addresses this gap.

---

## Target Customers

### Primary Buyers
- Medium to large enterprises using Microsoft Sentinel
- Regulated industries (finance, telecom, healthcare, government)
- Organizations with mature identity and email security controls

### Primary Users
- SOC analysts
- Incident responders
- Security engineering teams

### Secondary Stakeholders
- Security leadership (CISO, SOC managers)
- Risk and compliance teams
- IT operations

---

## Value Proposition

SARE delivers value by:

- Reducing mean time to triage (MTTT)
- Improving decision consistency
- Preventing unsafe automation
- Increasing analyst confidence in AI-assisted workflows
- Enabling controlled automation at scale

SARE does **not** replace analysts — it amplifies them.

---

## Differentiation

| Capability | Traditional SOAR | AI-only Tools | SARE |
|----------|------------------|--------------|------|
| Deterministic logic | Limited | ❌ | ✅ |
| AI assistance | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Governance & approvals | Basic | ❌ | ✅ |
| Auditability | Partial | ❌ | ✅ |
| Sentinel-native | Sometimes | ❌ | ✅ |

SARE’s key differentiator is **governed intelligence**.

---

## Why Sentinel Customers

Microsoft Sentinel customers already:
- Centralize security signals
- Use Logic Apps and KQL
- Require RBAC and auditability
- Operate in enterprise environments

SARE extends Sentinel’s strengths rather than competing with them.

---

## Ideal Use Cases

- Risky sign-in triage
- Phishing response coordination
- Privileged account protection
- High-volume alert normalization
- Analyst decision support

---

## Adoption Strategy

SARE is designed for:
- Incremental adoption
- Read-only insights first
- Approval-gated automation next
- Gradual expansion of execution scope

This reduces risk and builds trust.

---

## Key Takeaway

SARE enables organizations to scale security operations **without sacrificing control, safety, or accountability**.
